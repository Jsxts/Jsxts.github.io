因为 $m$ 只有 $5000$，考虑把每个 $F(a,b)$ 都求出来。

这个式子看起来没有什么可以进行的推理，考虑能否递推：

$$F(a,b)=\sum_{i=0}^b\dbinom{b}{i}\dbinom{n-i}{a}$$
$$F(a,b-1)=\sum_{i=0}^{b-1}\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i}{a}$$
$$F(a,b)-F(a,b-1)=\dbinom{n-b}{a}+\sum_{i=0}^{b-1}\left(\dbinom{b}{i}-\dbinom{b-1}{i}\right)\dbinom{n-i}{a}$$
$$=\dbinom{n-b}{a}+\sum_{i=0}^{b-1}\dbinom{b-1}{i-1}\dbinom{n-i}{a}$$
$$=\dbinom{n-b}{a}+\sum_{i=0}^{b-2}\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i-1}{a}$$
$$=\dbinom{n-b}{a}+\sum_{i=0}^{b-2}\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i}{a}-\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i-1}{a-1}$$
$$=\dbinom{n-b}{a}+\sum_{i=0}^{b-2}\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i}{a}-\sum_{i=0}^{b-2}\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i-1}{a-1}$$
$$=\dbinom{n-b}{a}-\dbinom{n-b+1}{a}+\sum_{i=0}^{b-1}\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i}{a}-\sum_{i=0}^{b-2}\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i-1}{a-1}$$
$$=F(a,b-1)-\dbinom{n-b}{a-1}-\sum_{i=0}^{b-2}\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i-1}{a-1}$$
$$=F(a,b-1)-\sum_{i=0}^{b-1}\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i-1}{a-1}$$

注意这里，考虑将后面的式子逆推回去：

$$\sum_{i=0}^{b-1}\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i-1}{a-1}$$
$$=\sum_{i=1}^{b}\dbinom{b-1}{i-1}\dbinom{n-i}{a-1}$$
$$=\sum_{i=1}^{b}\dbinom{b}{i}\dbinom{n-i}{a-1}-\sum_{i=1}^{b}\dbinom{b-1}{i}\dbinom{n-i}{a-1}$$
$$=F(a-1,b)-F(a-1,b-1)$$

所以

$$F(a,b)-F(a,b-1)=F(a,b-1)-F(a-1,b)+F(a-1,b-1)$$
$$F(a,b)=2F(a,b-1)-F(a-1,b)+F(a-1,b-1)$$

递推即可。
